## ⚠️ Read this before using the numbers

**1. The volumes are buckets, not measurements.** Every row falls into one of five
values — 50 000, 5 000, 500, 50, 0. That is Keyword Planner's low-resolution range
data, which is what Google returns when the account has no active spend. A "50" means
"somewhere in 10–100", not fifty.

| Bucket | Rows | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| 50 000 | 3 | 0.3 % |
| 5 000 | 14 | 1.4 % |
| 500 | 64 | 6.3 % |
| **50** | **864** | **84.5 %** |
| 0 | 73 | 7.1 % |
| no data | 4 | 0.4 % |

Only **81 rows (8 %)** sit above the floor bucket. The Step 1 rule of "50–100+ searches
per month" can only be enforced honestly against those. Everything selected below at
"50" is a *plausible* target, not a verified one. To get real numbers, run a small
Google Ads campaign for a few weeks, or pull the same terms through Ahrefs/SEMrush.

**2. The three phrases this site is actually built around returned no data at all:**

- `diseño web en medellín` — `--`
- `páginas web en medellín` — `--`
- `agencia de diseño web medellín` — `--`

They were added to the plan without Google having a bucket for them. Their absence is
not evidence of zero demand — the unaccented equivalents below do have volume — but it
does mean **we cannot prove the site's flagship phrase has traffic** from this export.

**3. There are zero Bogotá keywords in the file.** Not one row matches "bogot". But
`es/diseno-web-bogota.html` is live and in the sitemap. That page currently has **no keyword
research behind it whatsoever** — see the open question at the bottom.

## Deliberately rejected

High volume, wrong intent — chasing these would bring traffic that never converts:

| Keyword | Vol | Why rejected |
|---|---:|---|
| `páginas web` / `paginas web` | 50 000 | Navigational/informational, no commercial intent |
| `páginas` | 50 000 | Meaningless as a target |
| `crear pagina web gratis` | 5 000 | **"gratis" = the opposite of a paying client** |
| `pagina web gratis` | 5 000 | Same |
| `crear pagina web` | 5 000 | DIY intent — they want to build it themselves |
| `landing page que es` | 5 000 | Definition-seeking |
| `web dinamica` | 5 000 | Informational |
| `wix …`, `canva …`, `wordpress …`, `google sites …` | 500 ea. | Platform-shoppers, not agency-shoppers |

The whole "gratis / crear / wix / canva" cluster is the largest volume block in the
export and is worth **nothing** to an agency selling custom builds.

## Where exact match was deliberately not forced

The guardrail is explicit: if forced to choose between a keyword and natural language,
natural language wins. Three pages keep a header that does **not** contain the target
verbatim, because the exact phrase is stilted Spanish:

| Page | Target | Kept instead | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `es/medellin/seo-local-medellin.html` | `posicionar pagina web en google` | H1 stays "SEO local en Medellín"; target moved to an H2, "Cómo posicionar tu página web en Google" | The H1 is the page's own strong term. The H2 carries the target with a natural "tu". |
| `es/medellin/cuanto-cuesta-…html` | `precio crear pagina web` | "Rangos de precio para crear una página web en Medellín" | The bare phrase is not how anyone writes Spanish; the H1 question matches how people actually search. |
| `es/blog/tienda-online-medellin.html` | `diseño de tiendas virtuales en medellin` | H1 stays; target placed on the closing CTA header | Buyer-intent term belongs where the reader is asked to hire, not on an informational H1. |

Also note `es/diseno-web-medellin.html` keeps "Diseño Web en Medellín" in its **H1** (preserving the
role split against `es/index.html`) while its **title** now leads with the measured
500-volume term. The page owns both.
